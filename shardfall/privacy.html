Shards of Kesh Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Shards of Kesh is a strategy game. This policy explains what information the Android app handles. The short version: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. Shards of Kesh has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The developer does not run any servers that the game talks to.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your settings, campaign progress and the list of cosmetic items you own in the app's private storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Purchases

All gameplay is free. Optional cosmetic items (such as unit colors) can be bought through Google Play. Payments are handled entirely by Google; the developer never sees your name, email, payment card or billing address. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that an item was purchased so it can unlock it. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The app requests only the permissions it needs to run and to use Google Play Billing (internet access and the billing permission). It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Shards of Kesh is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
